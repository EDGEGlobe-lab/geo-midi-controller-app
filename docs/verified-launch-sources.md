# Verified Launch Source Notes

## Apple Mac mini (2024, M4)

- **Official launch announcement:** Apple Newsroom, dated 29 October 2024. Apple stated that the new Mac mini was available to pre-order on that date and would be available on 8 November 2024.
- **Official specification sheet:** Apple Support identifies the model as introduced in 2024 and lists an Apple M4 option with a 10-core CPU, 10-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine, 16GB unified memory, and 256GB SSD base storage.
- **Use on PARKWAY launch surface:** Present it as a third-party compatible compact DAW host, not a PARKWAY product, endorsement, licence, or supported-device guarantee.

## Ableton Move

- **Official launch post:** Ableton’s 8 October 2024 announcement describes Move as a compact standalone tool for music making and directs readers to buy it.
- **Official specification sheet:** Ableton lists a 1.5 GHz quad-core ARM Cortex-A72 processor, 2GB RAM, 64GB internal SD-card storage, 32 polyphonic-aftertouch pads, USB-C, USB-A MIDI, 3.5mm stereo input/output, a built-in microphone and speaker, plus Wi-Fi.
- **Use on PARKWAY launch surface:** Present it as a third-party compatible standalone music device. Do not imply that PARKWAY registers, licenses, controls, or is endorsed by Ableton or that it grants a Live licence.
