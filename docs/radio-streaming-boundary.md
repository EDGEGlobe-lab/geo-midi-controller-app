# PARKWAY Radio Workspace Boundary

The PARKWAY Radio workspace is an original browser player and station-discovery surface. It is not iHeartRadio, Rova, MediaWorks, or an affiliate, and it does not embed, relay, scrape, or claim control of their streams, accounts, brands, catalogues, or broadcasts.

Official iHeart terms identify its sites as including streaming services and other content governed by separate terms. Rova’s terms identify MediaWorks as the operator and describe live New Zealand radio streams and curated playlists as its services. Those sources establish why the PARKWAY interface must not present third-party radio content as its own or substitute project-owned previews for a licensed broadcast feed.

Initial PARKWAY stations use only project-owned playable audio previews already stored in the project. Each card states **PROJECT PREVIEW · NOT A LIVE BROADCAST**. A future external stream requires written authorization, a documented source URL, usage/territory rights, metadata provenance, and a server-controlled integration; no stream credentials are stored in the client.
