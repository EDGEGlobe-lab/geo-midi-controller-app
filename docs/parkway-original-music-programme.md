# PARKWAY Original Music Programme

## Purpose and provenance

This programme defines **20 original instrumental PARKWAY tracks**, each targeted at five minutes. Each finished track is composed from two generation clips with shared tempo, key, core instrumentation, and production profile. The generation system's approximate three-minute per-clip limit means the finished master should use a 151-second opening clip and a 150-second closing clip with a one-second crossfade, trimmed to 300 seconds after assembly.

Every stored asset must carry the following provenance metadata: `parkway-original-programme`, `user-approved-generation`, `instrumental`, `two-clip-five-minute-master`, and `no-third-party-source-supplied`. The tracks may use words such as *signal*, *wave*, *constellation*, and *receiver* as **abstract artistic language only**. They must not claim RF transmission, physics simulation, external media ingestion, or hardware control.

## Clip continuity contract

For each track, clip A and clip B must retain the same global directives: **instrumental only, no vocals**, stated BPM and key, core genre, principal bass/drum palette, wide but mono-compatible stereo image, clean master with controlled low end, and no third-party samples or recognisable copyrighted melodies. Clip A ends at the target energy and instrumentation state used for clip B's opening; clip B is then crossfaded and trimmed into the final 5:00 project asset.

## Generation controls

Generation remains user-visible and batch-controlled. The interface must show a pending, generating, assembling, completed, or failed state for each track. A completed master becomes selectable only after it is stored as a user-owned project asset with its provenance tags and duration metadata.
